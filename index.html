Season notes

  • El Niño is strong and still strengthening (NOAA Climate Prediction Center, Oct 8, 2026). It raises wind shear over the Atlantic, which tears developing storms apart.
  • That is why 2026 has been quiet: 9 named storms, but Isaias on Oct 8 was the first hurricane, the latest first hurricane since 1905.
  • Hurricane season runs through Nov 30. Late-season storms often form in the western Caribbean and Gulf and head toward Florida.
